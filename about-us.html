About Us

Maacro Pierce Company, Inc is a financial investment services company headquartered in New York, USA, specializing in providing investment advisory, market access support, trade execution assistance, and customized capital market solutions to institutional investors, professional asset management firms, family offices, and qualified corporate clients. The company’s business spans primary and secondary markets, primarily covering equities, funds, futures, and related alternative assets. It focuses on institutional trading support, investment research, cross-border capital allocation coordination, and long-term investment opportunity matching to establish a professional service system for different types of institutional clients. We do not primarily engage in individual retail business, but focus on deep, long-term cooperation with major financial institutions, emphasizing service quality, professional depth, and partnership stability.

The core team has long been active in the US and major international capital markets. Team members have experience in institutional trading, asset management, investment research, risk management, and cross-border investment. Some members have previously worked at well-known financial institutions. The company’s core management and business team members average more than 15 years of financial market experience. The team implements a multi-department collaboration mechanism among research, trading support, client service, and risk management, ensuring service response efficiency and consistency of professional judgment through regular cross-department meetings and shared information platforms.

Currently, the assets under management supported by Maacro Pierce-related services are approximately $8 billion, with about 400+ core personnel globally. The company is headquartered in New York, USA, and has established branch offices and business bases in North America, Asia-Pacific, and Europe, forming a cross-regional institutional service network. Regional bases mainly undertake local client liaison, market information collection, and regional resource coordination functions, while core decision-making and research support remain centered at headquarters.

The company’s development is built on long-term capital market experience, institutional investment discipline, and a robust risk management culture. Business origins can be traced back to 1998. Founder Sonny Pierce began related institutional investment services work at the age of 40. Today, what Maacro Pierce continues is not only a history starting from 1998 and formally established as a company in 2014, but also a long-formed institutional culture: discipline, independent judgment, risk awareness, and long-term value.
