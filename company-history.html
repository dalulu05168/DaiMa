The company’s business origins can be traced back to 1998. Founder Sonny Pierce began related institutional investment services work at the age of 40, by which time he had accumulated more than fifteen years of experience in the capital markets. Early business mainly revolved around private capital consulting, institutional investment advice, and US capital market services.

After years of accumulation and gradual business maturity, the company was formally registered and established as Maacro Pierce Company, LLC in early 2014. After the founder passed away in 2019, the company completed the management transition and continued to advance professional operations. From 2020 to the present, it has further strengthened institutional trading support, investment research, and cross-border cooperation capabilities, expanding business bases in Asia-Pacific and Europe.

Today, what Maacro Pierce continues is not only a history starting from 1998 and formally established as a company in 2014, but also a long-formed institutional culture: discipline, independent judgment, risk awareness, and long-term value.
