Investment Capabilities

THREE COMPLEMENTARY CAPABILITIES
01

Private markets

Strategic investment support, growth capital matching and private market allocation assistance.

02

Institutional markets

Market access and execution assistance through institutional channels and regional coordination.

03

Quantitative support

Data analysis, systematic research models and risk management frameworks.

Maacro Pierce’s investment and market services mainly revolve around three core areas: private market and strategic investment support, institutional markets, and quantitative strategy support. All services emphasize compliance, transparency, and executability, and are adapted according to the client’s internal processes and decision-making systems.

Institutional markets provide assistance such as block trades, equities and ETFs, funds, futures, digital assets, and cross-market trade execution for qualified clients through professional market networks, institutional trading channels, and execution support teams. Quantitative strategy support applies data analysis and systematic research models to global capital markets.
