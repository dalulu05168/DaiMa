Governance & Compliance

The company has systematically improved internal compliance and risk review processes and established a risk management framework covering the entire process of research, execution, and client service, ensuring that all services are carried out robustly under applicable regulatory frameworks.

Before services begin, the team needs to fully understand the client’s objectives, constraints, and risk tolerance. Service structures match the client’s internal approval processes and compliance requirements. Regional offices operate under the same overall compliance and risk standards.
