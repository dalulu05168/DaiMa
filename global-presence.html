Global Presence

A CROSS-REGIONAL SERVICE NETWORK

The company is headquartered in New York, USA, and has established branch offices and business bases in North America, Asia-Pacific, and Europe, forming a cross-regional institutional service network.

North America: New York (Headquarters), Chicago, Boston

Asia-Pacific: Singapore, Hong Kong, China

Australia: Sydney

Regional bases mainly undertake local client liaison, market information collection, and regional resource coordination functions, while core decision-making and research support remain centered at headquarters. Relying on these bases, the company can provide localized support and cross-regional resource coordination for institutional clients in different regions.
