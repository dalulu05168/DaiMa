Institutional Trading / Institutional Trading Platform

Institutional market business is one of Maacro Pierce’s core capabilities. Through professional market networks, institutional trading channels, and execution support teams, the company provides institutional market trading and execution assistance services for qualified clients. Mainly including:

Block Trades

Through institutional market networks, connect qualified clients with block trades, negotiated trades, and specific liquidity opportunities, and assist in completing execution coordination and process tracking.

Equities and ETFs

Cover matching and execution support for equity and ETF trading opportunities in the US and major international capital markets, focusing on price discovery, execution quality, and process transparency.

Funds

According to client asset allocation needs, assist in connecting with qualified funds and professional investment products, and provide relevant information support and preliminary screening assistance.

Futures

Participate in related opportunities in major international futures markets and provide support and coordination in conjunction with risk control requirements.

Digital Assets

According to applicable regulatory frameworks, assist in participating in digital asset markets and related institutional liquidity opportunities, strictly following compliance requirements and risk review processes.

Cross-Market Trade Execution

Combine market liquidity, price structure, and client trading needs to design corresponding execution assistance plans, track execution results, and provide post-trade analysis when necessary.

We emphasize maintaining full communication with clients during the service process to ensure that execution plans match the client’s risk preferences, internal approval processes, and compliance requirements. For complex or cross-market needs, the company coordinates different regional teams for joint support.
