Maacro Pierce applies data analysis, systematic research models, and risk management frameworks to global capital market research support. The company’s quantitative research system currently covers more than 350+ stocks and ETFs. Through continuous analysis of market structure, trading volume, liquidity, volatility, capital flows, risk exposure, and market correlations, a data-driven investment research and decision support framework is established.

The quantitative support system does not rely solely on predicting market direction, but emphasizes model discipline, research quality, and risk adjustment under different market environments, assisting institutional clients in improving research efficiency and decision robustness. Relevant analysis results are usually provided to clients in the form of reports, data summaries, or regular communications, facilitating direct use or further processing by their internal teams. The company also makes certain customized adjustments to the research framework according to client needs.
