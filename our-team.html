Maacro Pierce’s core team has long been active in the US and major international capital markets. Team members have experience in institutional trading, asset management, investment research, risk management, and cross-border investment. Some members have previously worked at well-known financial institutions. The company’s core management and business team members average more than 15 years of financial market experience.

There are approximately 400+ core personnel globally. The team implements a multi-department collaboration mechanism among research, trading support, client service, and risk management, ensuring service response efficiency and consistency of professional judgment through regular cross-department meetings and shared information platforms. For important institutional relationships, dedicated liaison personnel or small service teams are usually arranged.
