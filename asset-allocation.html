Maacro Pierce focuses on private market investment opportunities with long-term structural growth potential. Key research and support areas include fintech, clean energy and energy transition, healthcare, artificial intelligence, digital infrastructure, and emerging technologies.

Through strategic investment support, growth capital matching, and private market allocation assistance, the company is committed to helping institutional clients identify investment opportunities with long-term value creation potential outside traditional public markets. It typically combines the client’s asset allocation objectives and risk preferences to provide preliminary screening, due diligence coordination, information organization, and subsequent tracking support, rather than simply recommending projects. During the service process, emphasis is placed on information transparency and risk point sorting to assist the client’s internal team in completing decisions.

In 2022, Maacro Pierce assisted an institutional client in participating in a strategic investment in a US clean energy-related enterprise. The initial support scale was approximately $12 million. As the enterprise’s business progressed and subsequent capital entered, the project achieved approximately 1.8x value growth within about two years. During the process, the company assisted the client in completing key information matching, industry background sorting, and identification of major risk points, and provided phased progress tracking after the investment.
